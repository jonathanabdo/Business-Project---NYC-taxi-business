BEHIND THE ANALYSIS

A business question.
A traceable answer.

Which months and pickup areas contribute the most fare revenue, and how much of the change comes from trip volume versus the average fare?

Python prepares the data and calculates the summaries. This page displays those results and applies your filters in the browser.

What does one row represent?

One recorded green taxi trip. The source files include pickup time, location, distance, fare, tip, payment type and other charges. This covers green taxis, not every NYC taxi or ride-hailing service.

Which trips appear in the dashboard?

Loading…

We include card/cash records with positive fare and total amounts and a pickup date in the file’s own month. All source records remain in the downloadable project. Exact duplicates are counted but retained because there is no unique trip ID. We do not trim high fares. Distance outliers exist, so distance is not a dashboard KPI.

What do the money columns mean?

Base fares are the recorded fare_amount, excluding separate tips, taxes, tolls and surcharges. Passenger charges use total_amount and include the charges reported in the trip record, but exclude cash tips. Neither measure represents company profit or driver take-home pay.
