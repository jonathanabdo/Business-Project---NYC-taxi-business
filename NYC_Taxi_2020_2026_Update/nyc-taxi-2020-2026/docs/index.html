What does a trip count mean?

One included record represents one recorded green taxi trip, not one unique customer. This covers green taxis only.

Which trips are included?

Card/cash records with positive fare and total amounts whose pickup month matches the source file. The same rules apply to every year. No deduplication or upper-value trimming is applied; the source has no unique trip identifier. Records outside these rules are excluded, so these totals are not all TLC activity.

What do the money columns mean?

Base fares use fare_amount, excluding separately recorded tips, taxes, tolls and surcharges. Passenger charges use total_amount, including reported extras but excluding cash tips. Recorded tips exclude cash tips. These amounts are not profit or driver take-home pay. All values are nominal USD, without inflation adjustment.

How do I compare years fairly?

2020–2025 have 12 months each; 2026 has January–August. Use the annual chart’s January–August option for matching periods. Totals over all years include all 80 available months. Average fare is total base fares divided by total trips, not an average of annual averages. Fare per day uses calendar days in the selected months.

Where do the locations come from?

Pickup and drop-off location IDs are matched to the official TLC taxi-zone lookup. Unknown and outside-NYC locations remain included. Zone charts group the same fare receipts by origin or destination; do not add the two charts together.
